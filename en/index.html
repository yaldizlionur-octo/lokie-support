Lokie — temporary iPhone sharing

Lokie helps you manage temporary sharing sessions on your own iPhone. Choose the apps that stay available, supported web restrictions and a session duration. Screen Time authorization and available Face ID or Touch ID are required. Lokie does not lock the entire phone or remotely manage another device.

In a normal authorized session, timer expiry does not automatically remove restrictions; return to Lokie and authenticate to end the session. System permissions and conditions can affect this behavior. Biometrics identify an enrolled device user, not their age or parental status.

The current App Store version has no account, subscription, advertising or analytics SDK. Session and selection information stays on the device; this release does not record audio or analyze games or chats. The app interface is currently Turkish. This English page provides product information and support links. See the bilingual privacy and support pages for details, including website hosting and email handling.
